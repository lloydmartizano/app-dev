# My Favorite Movie
## The Intern
**Genre:** Comedy, Drama
**Director:** Nancy Meyers
**Release Year:** 2015

### About the Movie
The Intern is a movie about a 70-year-old man named Ben Whittaker who becomes a senior intern at an online fashion company. He works with the company's young CEO, Jules Ostin, and eventually becomes someone she can trust and learn from.

### Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because it shows the importance of hard work, patience, and learning from people with different experiences. It also teaches us that age does not stop someone from learning new things or making a difference.

### My Favorite Character
**Ben Whittaker** – I like his character because he is respectful, responsible, and always willing to help others.

### My Favorite Lesson
One of the lessons I learned from this movie is that experience and kindness can make a big difference in the workplace and in our daily lives.
